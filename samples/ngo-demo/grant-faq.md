# Grants & Donor Reporting FAQ

> organisational data.

## When is the quarterly report due?

Narrative and financial reports are due 30 days after the end of each quarter.
Submit the narrative to the programme manager and the financial report to
finance; the grants officer compiles and files the final package.

## What receipts do we need?

Every expense over 25 CHF needs a receipt. For cash purchases under 25 CHF, a
signed petty-cash voucher is acceptable. Bank transfer fees are reclaimable if
the donor's agreement allows them.

## Can funds be moved between budget lines?

No — budget lines are fixed by the donor agreement. Reallocations over 10% of a
line require a written donor approval before the expense is committed. Ask the
grants officer to prepare the request.

## What is the indirect-cost rate?

The standard indirect-cost rate is 7% of direct programme costs, unless the
donor agreement specifies a different ceiling. Confirm the rate with finance
before drafting a proposal budget.

## Reporting a delay

If a deliverable will be late, tell the grants officer at least two weeks before
the deadline. The donor expects advance notice; a late notification can put the
next tranche at risk.
