## Scope

This policy applies to all personal data we hold about beneficiaries, donors,
staff, and partners. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP)
and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where EU residents are
involved.

## Data minimisation

Collect only the personal data that is necessary for a specific, stated purpose.
Delete it when that purpose is complete and no legal retention obligation
remains.

## Retention

- Beneficiary records: 3 years after the last service, then securely deleted.
- Financial records: 10 years (statutory).
- Donor contact details: kept only while consent is valid.

## Confidentiality

Donor and beneficiary data is confidential. Share it internally only on a
need-to-know basis. Never send personal data to a third-party service that
stores or processes it outside the organisation without a signed agreement.

## Breach reporting

A personal data breach must be reported to the data protection officer within
24 hours. If the breach risks harm to individuals, we notify the supervisory
authority and, where required, the affected individuals without undue delay.

## Subject access requests

Anyone may ask what data we hold about them. Respond within 30 days. Provide a
copy, and correct or delete data where the request is valid.
