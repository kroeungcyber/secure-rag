## Collecting beneficiary data

When you register a beneficiary in the field, record only the minimum
information the intake form asks for. Never collect more than you need — this is
data minimisation and it protects the people we serve.

Required fields: full name, date of birth, village or commune, and the service
requested. Optional fields stay optional.

## Working offline

Field visits often have no internet. Complete intake on paper or in the offline
app, then sync when you return to the office or reach a connection. Never email
beneficiary data over an unsecured connection.

If the offline app shows "sync pending", do not delete the local copy. Hand the
device to IT support, who will run the sync and confirm the records uploaded.

## If someone asks about their data

A beneficiary has the right to ask what we hold about them and to ask us to
correct or delete it. Write down the request, note the date and the person's
full name, and pass it to your team lead the same day. Do not try to change
records yourself.

## Who to contact

- Lost or broken device: IT support (it-support@example-ngo.org)
- Data protection question: the data protection officer
- Urgent safety concern: your team lead first, then the field coordinator

## Emergency procedures

In a medical or safety emergency, prioritise the person's immediate safety
before paperwork. Record the incident as soon as it is safe to do so, and
notify your team lead within the same day.
